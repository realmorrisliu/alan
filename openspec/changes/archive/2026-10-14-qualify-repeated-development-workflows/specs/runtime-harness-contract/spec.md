## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Repeated development qualification exercises a real model through the native product entry
A repeated development qualification SHALL use the supported Alan Shell entry
and its Root Agent Process with real generation and explicit disposable project
authority. It SHALL freeze at least three repetitions of each of five families
on macOS and Linux: small repair, cross-file change, failure correction,
cancel/revoke/recover, and long-output follow-up. Scripted Tool tests, mocked
execution and earlier terminal-only acceptance MUST remain separate evidence.

#### Scenario: A full task is exercised
- **WHEN** a frozen task is submitted through a supported native terminal Host
- **THEN** its real model chooses Tools and authors the requested changes through product boundaries
- **AND** the harness checks the declared behavior and exact source/artifact/effect boundaries independently of the final answer

#### Scenario: A platform or provider prerequisite is unavailable
- **WHEN** actual model, runtime, grant or enforcing-backend readiness prevents a run
- **THEN** the original slot records the blocked or unsupported outcome and its reason
- **AND** no mock, silent model/tool substitution or portable test pass replaces native qualification

### Requirement: Development qualification retains first outcomes and intervention evidence
Qualification SHALL retain every frozen slot and original attempt, effective
source/binary/model/effort and environment identity, elapsed time, human
interventions, durable evidence references and exact verified effects. Planned
authorization/lifecycle actions SHALL be distinguished from corrective steering
or operator-authored solutions. Retries MUST link to the first outcome without
replacing it; absent evidence MUST remain unknown rather than inferred success.

#### Scenario: A task succeeds after intervention or a repair
- **WHEN** an operator supplies corrective advice, edits source or repairs an environment before successful completion
- **THEN** the original outcome and each intervention remain recorded alongside the corrected attempt
- **AND** assisted completion is distinguished from model-driven completion without unplanned intervention

#### Scenario: A run reports completion without required proof
- **WHEN** a final answer or zero command exit lacks the required behavior/effect, lifecycle or terminal evidence
- **THEN** qualification does not mark the slot passed
- **AND** unavailable usage/cost or missing retained details are reported explicitly

#### Scenario: Production source changes during qualification
- **WHEN** a discovered defect is repaired in a new candidate
- **THEN** the harness retains earlier-source outcomes and freezes the new source/binary identity
- **AND** affected rows are rerun and final qualification identifies the applicable final-candidate evidence for every slot

### Requirement: Development qualification preserves lifecycle and terminal evidence
Native qualification SHALL correlate Process lifecycle, AgentFS requests/actions,
rollout/checkpoint continuation and visible terminal state with verified effects.
Cancellation/revocation/recovery tasks MUST verify blocked later effects and
once-only completed effects through explicit recovery selection. Long-output
follow-up MUST inspect retained evidence and verify dependent work while keeping
input, detail navigation and scrollback usable under existing product contracts.

#### Scenario: Interrupted work resumes through explicit selection
- **WHEN** a frozen task cancels active work, revokes authority and later selects durable recovery
- **THEN** subsequent unauthorized effects remain blocked and restored work requires explicit valid authority
- **AND** completed effects do not replay and paused/waiting/failed/exit states remain distinct

#### Scenario: A follow-up depends on long Tool output
- **WHEN** stdout/stderr or a diff exceeds inline display bounds
- **THEN** the run checks retained detail or explicit loss, original result correlation and independently verified follow-up effects
- **AND** terminal captures verify the draft/cursor, detail return and usable scrollback without confusing literal text with generated role labels
